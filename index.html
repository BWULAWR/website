About the Review

Est. 2025

Serious engagement with the law does not require a law degree — only careful reading, honest argument, and a willingness to be edited.

The Bowdoin Undergraduate Law Review is a new journal, founded in 2025 and published by the Bowdoin Pre-Law Society. It will publish original legal scholarship written and edited entirely by undergraduates — constitutional law, jurisprudence, legal history, law and society, and international law, with a standing interest in the legal questions particular to Maine and the Northeast.

The Review has not yet published an issue. The founding board is being assembled now, and the editorial policy — how submissions are reviewed, which citation standard we hold to, when the deadlines fall — will be set by that board and published here in full before we open for submissions.

Volume I

In preparation

No issue has been published yet. Volume I is in preparation, and its date will be announced once the founding board has set it.

Nothing will appear on this page that has not been written by an undergraduate and read by our editors. When the first issue is published, it will be archived so that the articles in it remain citable permanently.

Submissions

Not yet open

Submissions are not open. When they are, the Review will accept work from undergraduates at any institution, and papers written for coursework will be welcome — in practice that is most of what journals like this publish.

Full guidelines — length, citation style, the review process, and the deadlines for each cycle — will be posted here before the first call for papers. If you would like to be told when that happens, write to submissions@bowdoinlawreview.org.

Join the Review

The founding board is forming now. No prerequisites and no prior legal coursework — staff editors read submissions, write comments, and check citations.
