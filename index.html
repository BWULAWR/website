About the Review

Est. 2025

Serious engagement with the law does not require a law degree. It does require careful reading, an honest argument, and a willingness to be edited.

The Bowdoin Undergraduate Law Review is a new journal, founded in 2025 and published by the Bowdoin Pre-Law Society. It will publish original legal scholarship written and edited entirely by undergraduates—constitutional law, jurisprudence, legal history, and international law, with a standing interest in the legal questions particular to Maine and the Northeast.

The Review has not yet published an issue. Its founding editorial board was seated in 2026 and is now setting the Review's editorial policy. How submissions are reviewed, which citation standard we hold to, and when the deadlines fall. That policy, and the masthead, will be published here before the Review opens for submissions in the 2026–27 academic year.
